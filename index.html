> COUNTERMEASURES — fight back

      

      

        ■ Fingerprint: use a browser that randomizes canvas/WebGL (Tor Browser, Brave, Firefox RFP)

        ■ Network identity: VPN or Tor hides your IP; your fingerprint can still betray you — fix both

        ■ Timezone + language: they geolocate you without GPS; Tor Browser normalizes both

        ■ Hardware specs: uncommon specs = unique you; common hardware or a normalized browser blends in

        ■ Behavior: extensions can inject noise into pointer/typing telemetry

        ■ The real fix: make surveillance expensive — look like everyone else.
